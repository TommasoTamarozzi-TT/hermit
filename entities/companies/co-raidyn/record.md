## Summary

Raidyn is a Leuven-based deep-tech company building AI-driven inspection and monitoring software for mission-critical engineering components. Its current public focus is steel wire rope monitoring through a product called **AURA**.

## Current State

- Public company launch appears to be recent, with third-party sources placing the company in 2024.
- The current public product focus is continuous **360° visual monitoring and inspection of steel wire ropes**.
- Raidyn presents its approach as a hybrid of **AI, computer vision, edge computing, and simulation-based modeling**.
- Public materials indicate early real-world deployments, several orders, and a growing pipeline, but exact customer counts and revenue are not public in the sources reviewed.

## Offering

- **AURA**: Automatic Rope Analysis / rope monitoring software.
- Core capabilities described publicly:
  - continuous 360° visual inspection
  - lay-length and diameter analysis
  - historical condition tracking
  - real-time monitoring from the control room
  - automatic and customizable reporting
  - integration with third-party magneto-inductive rope testing (**MRT**)
- Deployment posture:
  - designed to run on edge hardware
  - public references mention NVIDIA Jetson hardware
  - website positioning suggests local/industrial deployment rather than cloud-first monitoring

## Target Problems And Value

Raidyn's public messaging centers on:

- earlier detection of rope damage and degradation
- improved safety for mission-critical assets
- reduced downtime
- better inspection consistency and traceability
- support for predictive maintenance decisions

## Focus Industries

Publicly listed applications include:

- cableways
- mining
- offshore drilling
- construction

## Technology Positioning

Raidyn describes its technical edge as a combination of:

- applied AI
- computer vision
- hybrid simulation-data methods
- probabilistic performance models
- resource-efficient edge computing for industrial hardware

## Partnerships And Ecosystem

- Raidyn publicly presents the **AIM Consortium** / **Alliance for Intelligent Rope Monitoring** together with:
  - **Teufelberger-Redaelli**
  - **AMC Instruments**
- Public support and ecosystem signals:
  - NVIDIA Inception Program member since December 2024
  - supported by **VLAIO** through a PRISM-AI grant
  - supported by the **Vlaams Supercomputer Centrum (VSC)**
