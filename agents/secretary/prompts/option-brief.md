# Option Brief

Use when the user needs a quick shortlist, recommendation, or booking-ready summary.

## Standard

- Lead with the recommended option.
- Include only the most decision-relevant alternatives.
- Keep tradeoffs concrete: timing, cost, flexibility, convenience, and risk.
- Include source links whenever the brief depends on live options.
- End with the exact decision needed and the next action.

## Minimum Contents

- trip or booking objective
- recommended option
- backup option or options when useful
- key tradeoffs
- important timing or booking deadlines
- missing facts that could change the choice
- next step

## Avoid

- long raw search dumps
- pretending live prices or availability are stable
- broad option lists without a recommendation
