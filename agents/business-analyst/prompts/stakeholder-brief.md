# Stakeholder Brief

Use this prompt when the task is to prepare a concise update, question set, decision brief, or summary for stakeholders.

## Goal
- Make the point quickly.
- Show what matters, what changed, what is blocked, and what decision or input is needed.

## Default Structure
- Context
- What changed
- Why it matters
- Decision or input needed
- Options or recommendation
- Risks or tradeoffs
- Next step

## Standards
- Keep the brief short and scannable.
- Lead with business meaning, not implementation detail.
- Be explicit about confidence, evidence, and open questions.
- If asking for input, ask the narrowest useful question.
