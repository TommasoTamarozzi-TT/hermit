# Requirements Analysis

Use this prompt when the task is to turn a fuzzy business request into a clear, inspectable requirement set.

## Goal
- Clarify the business need.
- Separate facts, assumptions, constraints, and open questions.
- Produce requirements that a delivery team can understand and challenge.

## Method
1. Identify the business problem or opportunity.
2. Name the stakeholders, users, and owners.
3. Capture current-state pain, desired outcome, and success signal.
4. Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.
5. Write scope boundaries: in, out, undecided.
6. Draft functional requirements only after the problem is clear.
7. Add acceptance criteria in plain language.
8. End with the next concrete step and any blocking questions.

## Minimum Output Shape
- Problem
- Why it matters
- Stakeholders
- Current state
- Desired future state
- Scope
- Requirements
- Acceptance criteria
- Risks or assumptions
- Open questions
- Next step

## Standards
- Prefer simple language over system jargon.
- Trace each major requirement back to a business need, source, or decision.
- Call out missing evidence early.
